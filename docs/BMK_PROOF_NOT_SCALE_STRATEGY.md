# BADMAN KOMBAT — PROOF, NOT SCALE

## Strategic decision
We are not attempting to finance or imitate the production scale of a full AAA game. We are building a small number of extraordinary, original, executable moments that prove BMK's creative thesis and attract collaborators, audience and financing.

Money constrains volume. It does not set the ceiling on imagination.

## What must exist before a full-game conversation
### Clip 01 — THE PIT / Movie Becomes Playable
45–60 sec. Rain on zinc before picture. Leroy enters. Crowd recognises him. Marcus reveal. Air horn. Camera flows into gameplay. One excellent exchange. Heavy hit. KO hold.
Proof: visual identity, cinematic/gameplay handoff, combat feel, sound world.

### Clip 02 — WAIT... WAS THAT FOR ME?
45–75 sec. Establish an optional Player Echo detail early without emphasis. Later, named witness naturally uses it while also commenting on observed play style. Leroy briefly reacts. No explanation overlay.
Proof: Recognition + Reflection without surveillance.

### Clip 03 — THE CROWD REMEMBERS
60–90 sec. Show Fight A and a later return to The Pit. Named witnesses occupy evolved positions, allegiance has shifted, an object/environment detail changed, NPC references witnessed event, crowd reacts differently to Leroy's entrance.
Proof: persistent NPC/world memory.

### Clip 04 — SAME WORLD, DIFFERENT HISTORY
Split or sequential comparison of two play histories entering the same authored location. Same NPCs, but different greeting, trust, crowd pressure and remembered events.
Proof: personal fidelity rather than procedural randomness.

### Clip 05 — LAST STAND
30–45 sec gameplay-only capture. Leroy near defeat. Sound narrows. Rain/breath/feet become prominent. Heavy counter: hit-stop, body thump, haptic event hook, crowd eruption, sound system returns.
Proof: sensory direction and premium combat feel.

## Golden rule
Never fake gameplay and label it gameplay. Cinematic concept footage, target renders and actual runtime captures must be labelled accurately in private/public materials.

## Vertical-slice asset priority
1. Production Leroy hero GLB
2. Production Marcus/opponent GLB
3. The Pit hero environment kit
4. 8 named witness designs; only 2–3 need hero-quality models for initial clips
5. core animation set with excellent transitions

Everything else waits.

## Investor/audience story
Do not pitch map size, roster size or hundreds of missions. Pitch demonstrated inventions:
- Movie-to-Playable Handoff
- Player Echo
- Witness Memory
- Crowd Memory
- Living Rooms / environmental causality
- sensory combat direction
- Kingston 1986 specificity

## Evidence ladder
Idea → documented design → executable system → internal capture → tester reaction → public clip → audience signal → financing conversation.

Never skip from idea to grand claim.

## IP discipline
Keep dated commits, issues, design docs and captures. Separate public-facing names/concepts from implementation details. Before public campaign, run trademark availability checks for candidate product/system names and obtain professional IP advice on what is protectable by copyright, trademark, design rights, patent or trade secret. Do not publicly disclose patent-sensitive mechanics before advice if patent protection may be pursued.

## Success criterion
A person with no knowledge of TA GuruLabs watches one BMK clip and asks at least one of:
- 'What game is this?'
- 'When can I play it?'
- 'How did the NPC know that?'
- 'Who is making this?'

That curiosity is the first financing asset.
