# BADMAN KOMBAT — Vertical Slice Director's Cut

## Promise
The first 90 seconds must make the player feel they have entered a dangerous place with history, not clicked a web demo.

## 00:00–00:08 — Before the picture
Black. Rain on zinc. A distant sound system leaks through concrete. One crowd voice, indistinct. Bass arrives before image.

## 00:08–00:20 — Threshold
A warm practical flickers on. Shoes cross wet concrete. Camera stays low and refuses the face. The venue reveals itself in fragments: timber, zinc, speaker stack, condensation, spectators pressed close.

## 00:20–00:31 — Leroy
Camera rises behind Leroy. No superhero pose. Weight, breath, hands. The crowd recognition is heard before it is shown.

## 00:31–00:40 — Opponent reveal
Marcus is discovered late, across The Pit under cooler light. Music thins. The two silhouettes face one another. No menu interrupts the moment.

## 00:40–00:44 — Handoff
Air horn. BADMAN KOMBAT mark hits once. Camera moves, without a cut if possible, into the readable fight framing. ROUND ONE. Player owns Leroy.

## Combat grammar
- Light strike: fast, dry, readable.
- Heavy strike: anticipation, impact freeze, sub hit, displacement, brief camera impulse, crowd response.
- Guard: reduced damage but audible body pressure and crowd acknowledgement.
- Low health: music narrows; breathing and feet gain presence; HUD does not become a slot machine.
- Combo: crowd energy grows with credible sequences rather than arbitrary particle spam.
- Misses matter. Whiffs have recovery and vulnerability.

## Crowd as character
The crowd is not a looping background animation. It has energy, allegiance and memory. Player-favouring and opponent-favouring moments can shift its pressure. A KO should produce a peak, then a human half-second of consequence before celebration.

## KO
Do not immediately throw a victory panel over the body. Let the fall finish. Hold. Hear the room. Camera finds the winner and loser in the same world. Then allow the title/result layer to arrive.

## Visual grammar
Warm Kingston memory vs restrained cool future intrusion. Black, charcoal, wet concrete, dark skin, amber practicals, cyan only as an accent. Rain and haze catch light. Avoid rainbow neon.

## Sound grammar
Bass travels through architecture. Rain has location. Crowd has distance. Impacts have transient + body + room response. Music can duck to make violence feel larger. Silence is also an effect.

## Mobile
Thumb controls must disappear from attention, not compete with cinematography. Large touch targets, minimal labels, haptic-ready event hooks. Landscape is preferred for the hero fight; portrait may present a rotate-device cinematic card rather than a compromised arena.

## Non-negotiable
At no point should the experience feel like a webpage with a fighting game embedded inside it.
