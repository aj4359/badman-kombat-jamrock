# BMK Mythic Cosmology

## Principle
BadMan KomBat begins in a tactile, human Kingston. Mythic and cosmic elements are earned gradually rather than replacing the street-level world.

The progression is:

**STREET -> LEGACY -> MYTH -> COSMOS**

Kingston remains the emotional centre at every scale.

The principal threshold between the grounded and mythic layers is currently developed as **The Veil**. See `docs/BMK_THE_VEIL.md`. The Veil is original BMK mythology and must not imply connection to Marvel, Wakanda, Black Panther, or another third-party continuity without a negotiated licence.

## The Old Lion
The supplied heavy older fighter reference establishes a useful silhouette and physical language: mature, huge, weathered, grounded, dangerous without posing.

BMK will develop an original character from that archetype, working name **The Old Lion**. Do not reproduce an identifiable actor, costume, weapon arrangement, tattoos, or production design from any photographic reference.

Design language:
- heavyweight older Black fighter
- visible age and accumulated damage
- enormous hands, shoulders and forearms
- economical movement rather than bodybuilder posing
- weathered leather / workwear informed by BMK's own world
- authority comes from history, not fantasy ornament
- potentially a mentor, gatekeeper or late-game combatant

## Ancestral / Cosmic Faction
The supplied Afro-futurist royal-warrior images are visual ideation, not production characters. BMK should independently develop an original faction combining African diasporic memory, speculative technology and the game's own mythology.

Rules:
- no direct reproduction of supplied crowns, armour, weapons, symbols or faces
- research real traditions carefully rather than mixing cultures as decoration
- fictional symbols and technology must have in-world purpose
- avoid generic 'ancient aliens' exposition
- preserve mystery

Potential narrative function: the player initially interprets unusual events as rumours, hallucinations, underground technology or folklore. Certainty comes much later, if at all.

## The Lineage Field
The glowing DNA / Earth image suggests a system concept rather than literal pseudo-science.

Working term: **The Lineage Field**.

It represents inheritance across generations: stories, choices, techniques, consequences and community memory. It is not a claim that ethnicity, personality or destiny can be read from DNA.

Gameplay possibilities:
- fighting techniques learned from previous generations
- world-state consequences inherited by later chapters
- recurring symbols and musical motifs
- named witnesses passing memories onward
- late-game visualisation of connected histories

This can connect to BMK's existing principle: **Each fighter carries history. Each battle carries memory.**

## Family / Generational Storytelling
The supplied family photograph suggests another powerful direction: BMK can tell stories across generations rather than treating every fighter as an isolated avatar.

If real family likenesses are ever used, obtain explicit permission from each recognisable person before production. Until then, photographs are private/reference inspiration only and are not source textures or face scans.

Possible structure:
- elders carry history
- current fighters carry unresolved consequences
- younger characters inherit stories but choose what to keep
- relationships affect how witnesses interpret fights

## Visual Escalation
### Tier 1: Kingston
Rain, zinc, timber, concrete, sound systems, street tailoring, practical light, human-scale violence.

### Tier 2: Legacy
Old gyms, hidden rooms, heirlooms, stories, scars, photographs, repeated techniques, witnesses who remember.

### Tier 3: Myth
Rare impossible-seeming events, symbolic spaces, unexplained fighters/factions, heightened perception. The Veil is the primary narrative threshold into this layer.

### Tier 4: Cosmos
Only after the player is emotionally invested in the human world. Scale may become planetary/cosmic, but the story must remain about people, memory, inheritance and choice.

## Canon Guardrails
- The current Kingston 1986 vertical slice stays grounded.
- No cosmic armour or supernatural spectacle is added to The Pit merely because it looks impressive.
- Mythic material belongs to later chapters, visions, legends or carefully earned reveals.
- No real-person likeness enters production without rights clearance.
- No third-party character, costume or franchise design is reproduced.
- Cultural specificity beats decorative mash-ups.
- Real Jamaican and Maroon history must be researched respectfully; fictional supernatural claims should not be attached to real communities or sacred locations.

## Production Consequence
Do not expand current GLB scope because of this document. Leroy remains first production hero asset, Marcus second. The Old Lion can enter concept development in parallel, while the mythic/cosmic faction remains narrative and visual-development work until the grounded vertical slice passes its quality gates.

For the financing proof, The Veil may be foreshadowed only through a tiny anomaly or post-credit beat. The impossible only matters after the real feels real.
