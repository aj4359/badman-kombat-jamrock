## World

## Music canon
Primary sonic identity: Jamaican Roots Reggae + Dub, especially the broad late-1970s/1980s production language.

Development rule: ORIGINAL/CLEARED MUSIC ONLY. Do not depend on commercial classic-reggae licences until project funding permits selective licensing.

Original BMK music may evoke the era but MUST NOT copy identifiable melodies, lyrics, hooks, basslines, samples, recordings or distinctive protectable elements of existing songs.

Core sonic principle: THE BASS ISN'T UNDERNEATH THE WORLD. THE BASS IS PART OF THE WORLD.

Dub is storytelling language: roots -> dub -> tension/combat -> space/silence -> return. Music should be diegetic where appropriate: sound systems, radios, record shops, cars, homes, clubs and turntables.

## Comic
BMK #0: BEFORE THE KOMBAT is the canonical digital prelude and first public doorway into the universe. Mobile-first, cinematic and interactive. It must create desire to play the game rather than explain every secret.

Initial proof sequence: headphones/entry -> Kingston establishing scene -> Leroy in rain -> approach to sound system -> confrontation tease -> mysterious final reveal.

## Physical artefact
BMK-OBJ-VINYL-K87-001 = BADMAN KOMBAT™ — KINGSTON '87. The record is seeded inside the digital story before becoming a real limited physical pressing. Its initial catalogue should be based primarily on original BMK Roots/Dub material.

## Cultural guardrails
No tourist-Jamaica shorthand, generic Caribbean styling, cyberpunk Jamaica, or stereotype-by-costume. Do not make a character 'more Jamaican' through random dreadlocks, Rastafarian colours or cannabis imagery. Historical uncertainty should be researched/flagged, not invented as fact.
