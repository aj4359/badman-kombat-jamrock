# BMK Takeover Build Plan

## Objective
Replace fragmented prototype development with a canon-led pipeline that supports the digital comic, game, cinematics, music and future merchandise from shared identities and data.

## Phase 0 — protect what exists
- Work on feature branches, not main.
- Treat current Phaser/prototype gameplay as legacy foundation, not final presentation.
- Do not delete legacy assets until replacements are verified.

## Phase 1 — Canon Registry
Implement machine-readable registries for characters, wardrobe, locations, music and canonical objects. All new story/game content references stable IDs.

## Phase 2 — Character Continuity Gate
Build approved model-sheet slots for Leroy, Asha, Marcus, Monk Wu and Elder Zion. Test Leroy across: rainy street / crowded sound-system dance / post-confrontation close-up. Expansion is blocked until identity is recognisably consistent.

## Phase 3 — BMK #0 Vertical Slice
Build six scenes:
1. Headphones recommended / Enter Kingston
2. Kingston, Jamaica — 1987
3. Leroy walking in rain
4. Sound-system approach
5. 'Badman reach' confrontation
6. Mystery / Kingston is only the beginning

Requirements: mobile first, progressive loading, reduced-motion path, captions, audio opt-in, graceful fallback.

## Phase 4 — AudioDirector
Create reusable audio states: AMBIENT / ROOTS / DUB / DANCE / TENSION / COMBAT / ESCAPE / SILENCE / MYTHOLOGY. Support stems/versions and diegetic source positioning. Development assets must be ORIGINAL_BMK or explicitly CLEARED.

## Phase 5 — Full Issue
Only after continuity + six-scene experience pass, expand BMK #0 to approximately 20–24 story scenes. No filler.

## Phase 6 — Game convergence
Reuse canon IDs, locations, music IDs and AudioDirector concepts in the playable game. Comic engineering must not become disposable marketing code.

## Acceptance gates
- Leroy remains recognisably the same person across scenes/media.
- Kingston feels inhabited and historically intentional.
- Roots/Dub is integral to narrative, not pasted-on soundtrack.
- No dependency on paid commercial music licences for v1.
- No legacy placeholder can overwrite canonical identity.
- Mobile experience remains usable with sound/effects disabled.
