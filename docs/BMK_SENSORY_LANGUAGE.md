# BADMAN KOMBAT — Sensory Language

BMK should communicate combat through multiple channels at once, but never drown the player in effects.

## Visual
Light attacks are mostly animation and displacement. Heavy attacks earn a short local flash, hit-stop and camera impulse. Low health subtly narrows perception rather than covering the screen with red graphics.

## Aural
Impacts have layers: transient contact, low body thump, room response, crowd response. Music is allowed to duck. Rain, breath, shoes and architecture become more audible as Leroy approaches danger.

## Haptic
Mobile vibration is punctuation, not constant buzzing.
- jab: single short tick
- heavy: three-stage impact pattern
- guard: tight double/triple pulse
- hurt: heavier single pulse
- KO: unmistakable decaying pattern

## Perception
Below roughly 35% health, field of view may tighten subtly and ambience/music can narrow. Below roughly 18%, enter Last Stand: breathing, footwork and opponent threat become perceptually dominant. Avoid fake blur that harms playability.

## Dramatic beats
neutral → pressure → danger → last stand → KO

These are directors, not scripted outcomes. They respond to the actual match state and should influence camera, sound, crowd and UI intensity without taking control away from the player.

## Accessibility
Every sensory channel must have an alternative. Haptics can be absent. Audio cues cannot be the sole source of critical combat information. Camera impulses and perception effects require intensity/reduction settings before public release.
