## Creative grammar
- Mature cinema noir: rain, deep shadow, practical street light, film grain, sweat, smoke, wet asphalt.
- Violence is fast, readable, consequential and character-led. Avoid empty gore-as-decoration.
- Use anticipation, silence, impact and aftermath.
- Dub mixing is part of the edit: roots -> dropout -> bass/drums -> silence -> impact -> dub return.
- Kingston and Jamaican culture remain the differentiator. No generic cyberpunk/tourist-Jamaica treatment.
